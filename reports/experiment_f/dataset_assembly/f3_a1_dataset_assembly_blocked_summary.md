# F3-A1 dataset assembly decision

Decision: `F3_A1_DATASET_ASSEMBLY_BLOCKED`.

The three approved F-ADAPTATION sessions are unambiguous and contribute 942 prospective rows labeled `DDoS` within the Amendment A1 scope of a controlled HTTP DDoS-like/load profile. No genuine distributed-DDoS claim is made.

Assembly stopped before reading rows into a candidate dataset because the frozen Experiment F design authorizes historical source categories but does not name exact historical artifacts, session IDs, row-selection rules, or row counts. The prior Experiment E candidate applied Experiment E-specific deterministic caps; Experiment F does not state whether to reuse that candidate or carry those caps forward. Selecting a composition from repository availability would invent a post-preregistration rule.

No candidate dataset, lineage table, split, inference, prediction, traffic, or RF-v4 model was created. Validation, runtime-validation, diagnostic, and final data were not inspected for assembly.

Next step: prospectively freeze the exact historical composition in an Experiment F amendment, then re-authorize dataset assembly.
